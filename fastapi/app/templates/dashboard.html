DCAI Houston Voice Bot Integration Guide
Welcome to the Clinic Management Dashboard. This guide outlines how the AI voice agent captures leads, simulates/places real calls, syncs with GoHighLevel (GHL) CRM, and handles booking approvals.
1
Lead Acquisition
Inbound / Outbound
Leads are captured via online ads or manual triggers. GHL pushes the details to our backend via webhooks, triggering an outbound Twilio call. Inbound calls are received directly from patients dialling the Twilio number.
2
AI Conversation
Gemini Live
The call connects directly to Gemini. The AI voice bot qualifies the patient, explains All-on-X details (without revealing pricing upfront), and checks for calendar slots.
3
Booking Request
CRM Booking Status
Once the patient selects a slot, the AI books it in GHL as a new request (Option 1). It saves the GHL appointment ID and displays the slot as pending in the dashboard.
4
Manual Review & Sync
Live API Action
The clinic team reviews the call transcript. Clicking **Confirm** updates the GHL appointment status to confirmed. Clicking **Decline** updates it to cancelled in GHL.
💡 Tips for Testing:
• Web Dialer: Use the Test Suite Dialer tab to start an inbound call simulation. Make sure to talk through booking a slot.
• MongoDB Handoff: View call details, listen to local recordings, and approve bookings directly from the Dashboard tab.
• Live GHL: Set your GHL_API_KEY in config.properties to sync approvals with your CRM in real time.

DCAI Portal System Info
Ideology & Business Flow
DCAI Houston matches dental implant candidates with All-on-X consultation slots. The AI voice agent answers candidate FAQs, checks slot availability, and requests bookings.
Campaign Flow (Option 1)
We implement the Appointment Request Workflow. Bookings are saved as pending requests. The doctor manually confirms or declines them, syncing back to the GoHighLevel API.
Dashboard Actions
  • Recent Interactions: Analyze live transcripts, sentiments, summaries, and play call recordings fetched locally.
  • Patient Directory: Manage records (DOB, ZIP, preference notes).
  • Appointments: Action buttons send PUT requests to confirm/cancel slots in GHL CRM.
